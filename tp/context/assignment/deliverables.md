# Deliverables checklist

## Two tasks

### Analysis and Validation

- Explore, analyse and prepare the dataset, extracting relevant knowledge for the
  problem.
- Obtain and critically analyse the modelling results.
- Perform thorough feature engineering and analyse the factors that most characterise
  biological age and profile anomalies, using model interpretability techniques.

### Competition

- Work on the Kaggle competition dataset; submit model results to obtain the Macro
  F1-score.
- **Metric:** macro F1 across the 16 age groups.
- **Submissions:** 3 valid submissions per day; invalid submissions do not count;
  only one file counts for the final ranking. Start submitting early.
- **Team name:** `GRUPO_<CURSO(s)>_<X>`, where `<CURSO(s)>` is one or more of
  MEI, MECD, MES, MIA, MMC, MCS, MCA, MRC and `<X>` is the group number.
- **Groups:** 2–4 members. An incomplete group cannot submit on Kaggle.
- Obtain and critically analyse the results; interpret them and explain their
  usefulness for the problem.

## Report

- Limited to **20 pages**.
- Required sections:
  1. Areas to be tackled, objectives, and how they will be achieved.
  2. Methodology used and how it was applied.
  3. Detailed dataset description and exploration, and all processing performed.
  4. Models developed: characteristics, hyperparameters tuned, training details.
  5. Summary of results and critical analysis.
  6. Identification and characterisation of the main profile anomalies (large
     discrepancies between real and predicted age).
  7. Suggestions and recommendations after analysing the results and models.
- The whole process must include examples and indications that make every step
  reproducible.

## Delivery

- Compress the report and all other elements into **one zip**.
- One group member submits it on e-learning under
  `Conteúdo/Instrumentos de Avaliação em Grupo/Submissão TPG`.
- Deadline: **2027-01-02**.

## Peer assessment

- Counts for **1 point** of each student's grade.
- Collective analysis of each member's contribution and effort.
- Each member's delta may be negative, zero or positive.
- Within a group, the deltas must sum to **0.00**.
- An individual delta must lie in **[−1.0, +1.0]**.
- One member sends the email; all other group members are in CC.
- Send to the **5 recipients** listed in `enunciado.md`.
- Subject: `AP DAA - GRUPO_<CURSO(s)>_<X> - Avaliação Por Pares`.
